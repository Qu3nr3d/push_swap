Normal tests

Works with:
* no arguments
./push_swap
* one number
./push_swap 5
* two numbers
./push_swap 12 5
* sorted array for each algorithm
./push_swap --simple 45 67 90 900
./push_swap --medium 45 67 90 900
./push_swap --complex 45 67 90 900
* bench
./push_swap --bench 12 5 --complex
./push_swap --bench --simple 12 5
* checks if is error
./push_swap 12 45 3 34 3
./push_swap 12 45 a 34 3
* more complicated stream
shuf -i 0-9999 -n 500 > args.txt ; ./push_swap --bench $(cat args.txt) 2> bench.txt
* numbers and arrays together
./push_swap "     12    5    " 45 '1' "  34 56"
./push_swap '12 34 56' '    34' "12 34 56" 45
./push_swap "12 45 2" '  5 2 3  12'
* arrays
ARG="4 67 3 87 23"; ./push_swap --complex $ARG | ./checker_linux $ARG
* complex z INT_MIN
./push_swap --complex 4 -34 12 -2000 -2147483648

* pluses and minuses in arrays
